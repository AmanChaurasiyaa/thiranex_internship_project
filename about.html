About me

I'm a second-year B.Tech Computer Science student at GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management, learning web development while keeping up a parallel practice in illustration.

My story

I started out drawing before I started coding, and I still treat the two the same way: sketch first, refine after. That habit carries into how I build interfaces — rough layout first, then attention to detail and polish.

Outside class, I've served as Class Representative for two years running and stayed active in student club activities, which is where I first started picking up organisational and communication skills alongside the technical ones.
